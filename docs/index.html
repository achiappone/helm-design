What was wrong

Each tooth is a box rolled 45°, which turns a TOOTH_H×1.5 square into a diamond ×√2 wider tangentially. At 20 teeth on r4.5–8.2 that made a 2.55 mm tooth sitting in a 1.41 mm pitch arc: adjacent teeth overlapped, and 97% of the crown fused into a plain ring. The build proved it — 20 teeth → 1 solid, not 20.

Two more errors were hiding behind it. The mesh gap was computed as TOOTH_H×1.25, forgetting the same 45° roll that makes a crown stand ×1.5607 proud — so every tooth tip buried 0.30 mm into the opposing solid. And the visor's hood ran the full span between the upstands, straight through where the shell's own crowns live.

Now: 16 teeth on r8–13, valley 1.08 mm, 50% duty. Both joints nest to zero overlap at half a pitch and peak at 119 mm³ on the tooth crests. That difference is the click.

Housing

Visor and bracket — rebuilt to the current pivots

Both parts now read every mating dimension from the housing rather than keeping copies. That is what let them drift: the visor had its pivot axis at y94/z12 against the shell's y87.75/z9, and its teeth at r5.0–9.5 against r4.5–8.2. Neither could ever have assembled.

Other printed parts

Also fixed on the way

Pivot axes were inside the walls

They sat OUT_H/2 + 4.5 above the wall against a 9.5 ear radius, so the bottom 5 mm of every ear was buried. Now derived as OUT_H/2 + R_EAR + 1.5, asserted.

Bottom blend block had a sign error

Its height read OUT_H/2 + BP_Y + 12 — the negative of the span it should be. It stayed positive only while the pivot offset was small; raising the axis drove it negative and the kernel threw.

The bigger ears fouled the cover — caught before printing

Enlarging the pivot ears for the detent pushed them to z 23.5 against a DEPTH of 22, and their blend blocks run inboard to OUT_H/2 − 12 — well inside the cover's footprint. That put 619 mm³ of shell in the cover's space, in four lumps; the cover would not have seated. Now guarded at the mating plane the same way the front face is, and asserted to zero. The ears themselves sit outboard of the cover and are left alone, which is why the shell still measures 24 tall.

My own measurement was lying

The visor imports as a Compound and the bracket as a Solid. A Location on a Compound wrapper is ignored by booleans, so every "rotated" visor I swept was actually the unrotated one. The assembly scripts now unwrap to solids before transforming.
